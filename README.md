# Agustina Cerri, Ph.D.

**Viral metagenomics · Bioinformatics · Virus discovery in bats and human samples**

- Human Virology Group, Institute of Molecular and Cell Biology of Rosario (IBR, CONICET-UNR)
- Virology Area, Microbiology Department, Faculty of Biochemical and Pharmaceutical Sciences, National University of Rosario (UNR)

## About

I discover and characterize novel viruses in bats and other wildlife (One Health framework) and in human samples, combining high-throughput sequencing (Illumina, Oxford Nanopore), viral metagenomics, sequence- and profile-based homology searches, and phylogenetic analysis. I build reproducible command-line pipelines in Bash and Python, and I teach bioinformatics at postgraduate level.
